2. The transformational five

Selected from the 17 total candidates by the creative-thinking frameworks. These are the ones that *change the rules* of SelTDA, not just refine the cascade. Each has a two-sentence pitch in 2026-05-26-creative-thinking-deepening.md.

EB-1 — Energy-Based SelTDA

Replace the 3-gate cascade with a single learned energy function Eφ(I,Q,A) trained contrastively against real labeled data. Unifies the gates and unlocks decades of EBM theory (calibration, phase transitions, RG).

Counter-evidence: Shevchenko 2022 (arXiv 2206.14355) found EBMs lag CL for VQA representation learning. Mitigation: frame as filter-calibration EBM, NOT representation EBM. Different scope.

RW-1 — Reward-Shaped VQG

Use the filter score as a DPO reward signal training the teacher in-loop, instead of post-hoc discarding. West-of-N style preference dataset construction.

Counter-evidence: Rafailov 2024 (arXiv 2406.02900) shows reward hacking in DPO is a real, measurable failure mode. Mitigation: held-out judge as auditor (GPT-4V or independent BLIP-ITM-Large); KL budget; track per-gate reward acquisition. This *characterization of hacking modes* becomes its own contribution.

MC-1 — MCMC-SelTDA

Reframe iterative SelTDA as Metropolis-Hastings sampling over the student-parameter posterior. Import R-hat, ESS, and burn-in diagnostics. Gives the first principled stopping criterion for self-training.

Confirmatory evidence: Karan & Du 2025 "Reasoning with Sampling" (arXiv 2510.14901, 49 HF upvotes) frames base-model improvement as MCMC without training. Direct precedent.

CS-X — Coreset-Stratified Filter (Janusian-synthesized from CS-1)

Resolve the "filter strictness ↔ data quantity" tension by recognizing that "quantity" = "informational coverage". Combine submodular coreset selection with per-question-type quantile filtering.

Confirmatory evidence: ZCore (Griffin et al., Nov 2024, arXiv 2411.15349) does exactly zero-shot coreset selection on unlabeled pools using foundation-model embeddings. Algorithmic fit is direct. Lowest-risk highest-yield candidate.

PV-1 — Pivot-VQG

Decouple question generation from answer generation. Stage-1 model produces only Q from image; stage-2 (possibly stronger) answers it. Filtering each stage independently improves quality and diversity.

Surfaced from NEG(C2): "Q and A are not generated jointly." Cheap mini-extension feasible in any iterative variant.

4. The xcons leakage finding (action required)

configs/filter_pseudo.yaml currently points xcons.student_ckpt at cache/student_weights/checkpoint_09.pth — which per CLAUDE.md is the published SelTDA student (trained on synthetic data). Spec §3.3 explicitly says the xcons judge must be the pretrained-only baseline, not a SelTDA-trained student.

Why it matters: a pseudo-QA whose answer matches the model's training distribution will score high on xcons regardless of correctness. The gate becomes a tautology and the H1 numbers are uninterpretable.

Fix options:

  1. Download BLIP-base pretrained weights (not in current dataset.sh — needs to be added) and point xcons there.
  2. Train a fresh A-OKVQA-real-only student (no synthetic) for use as the xcons judge.

Either is feasible. Option 1 is faster, but the BLIP-base pretrained checkpoint is large (~900MB). Option 2 takes ~10h A5000.

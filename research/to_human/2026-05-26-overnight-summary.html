6. Compute efficiency (filter + train)

research/notes/2026-05-26-compute-efficiency.md

Filter (~3–6h cho 51k QA, không ~45 phút)

  • conf — đọc JSON, instant
  • itm — CLIP × 51k, batch=1
  • xcons — BLIP beam-3 generate × 51k → bottleneck

Fix: scoring_only=true một lần; sweep threshold offline; pilot tắt xcons.

Train (~10h / variant / seed)

  • 10 epoch, ~34k samples/epoch, không early-stop val
  • H1 naive: 7×3×10h ≈ 210h chỉ train

Fix: successive halving — proxy max_epoch=3 + 1 seed → full train top-2 only; reuse checkpoint unfiltered.

H1 revised budget: ~85–100h thay vì ~260h.
